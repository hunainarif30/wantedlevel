Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Wanted Level website and Wanted Level Poster, an internal tool the Wanted Level team uses to publish short video clips to its own social media accounts.

Who uses the app

Only the Wanted Level team. The app is not offered to the public, and it only connects to accounts owned and operated by Wanted Level (TikTok @wantedlevelshorts, Instagram @wantedlevelshorts, the YouTube channel "Wanted Level" and the Facebook Page "Wanted Level").

What data the app uses

  • Access tokens that TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta issue when we authorise the app for our own accounts. They are used only to publish our videos and read our posts' statistics.
  • Basic profile information of our own accounts (account ID, username) to confirm we post to the right account.
  • Statistics of our own posts (views, likes) to measure which clips perform.

The app does not collect data about viewers or any other users, and it does not read messages, followers' personal data or other people's content.

Where data is stored

Tokens and post statistics are stored only on the team's own computer, in local files that are not shared or uploaded anywhere else. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use and can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta: Settings › Business integrations). Revoking a token immediately stops the app's access; local files are then deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @wantedlevelshorts on TikTok.
