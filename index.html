Wanted Level Poster (our app)

Wanted Level Poster is an internal publishing tool used only by the Wanted Level team. It takes short vertical clips that we edited from streams we have permission to clip, and publishes them to our own Wanted Level accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook at scheduled times, with the caption, hashtags and disclosures each campaign requires. It also reads the view counts of our own posts so we can see which clips perform.

It does not access, collect or post to anyone else's account.
